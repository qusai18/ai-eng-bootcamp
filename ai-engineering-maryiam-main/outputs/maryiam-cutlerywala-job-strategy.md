### Market and Competitive Analysis
As of mid–late 2026, Business Analyst demand remains material but competitive, especially for fully remote roles. Get U Hired’s 2026 BA hiring snapshot reported ~39,264 unique BA postings over ~89 days (~3,013/week), with ~70% aimed at mid–senior talent; only ~31% offered remote or hybrid flexibility, and ~14% were fully remote ([Get U Hired — Business Analyst Hiring Trends 2026](https://getuhired.co/insights/roles/business-analyst/)). Skillenai’s index of ~3,570 “Business Analyst” postings in the 90 days ending 2026-09-13 listed SQL, Agile, user stories, Jira, and requirements gathering among the most-cited skills ([Skillenai — BA jobs 2026](https://skillenai.com/data/role/business-analyst)).

Broader remote-work data is tougher still: Robert Half’s Q1 2026 posting analysis found ~77% of new postings fully on-site, ~19% hybrid, and only ~4% fully remote — confirming Maryiam’s instinct that market conditions for remote BA roles are scarce and contested ([Robert Half — Remote work statistics and trends for 2026](https://www.roberthalf.com/us/en/insights/research/remote-work-statistics-and-trends)). A Remote Job Assistant review of 312 remote BA postings (Oct 2025–Mar 2026) found Agile/Scrum in ~78%, SQL in ~61%, and Jira/Confluence in ~54%, with median remote BA base often cited around $95K–$105K; healthcare, consulting, and government contracting were called out as comparatively accessible lanes versus FAANG-style screens ([Remote Job Assistant — Remote BA jobs](https://www.remotejobassistant.com/blog/remote-business-analyst-jobs)).

Implication for this search: treat “fully remote + mid-level BA” as a constrained funnel. Win by specificity (delivery case studies, Agile/UAT proof, stakeholder liaison) and channel quality (LinkedIn narrative + targeted applications), not by volume alone. Prefer employers with established remote BA cultures (distributed SaaS, certain consultancies, select healthcare/gov-contract roles) over generic “remote OK” listings that later force onsite.
